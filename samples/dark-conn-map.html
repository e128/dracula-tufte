Large map

Past roughly 15 to 20 nodes on one rank the small map's flat fan-out sprawls (see NOTES.md, Large maps). This map groups antecedents and descendants into open subgraphs, switches to the ELK layout engine, and states each relationship style once in a legend instead of on every edge. Every node stays clickable: the subgraphs are never collapsed, because view: collapsed drops a cluster's nodes, and their links, from the render entirely. A consumer wanting real per-node links adds a click directive per node and sets window.mermaidSecurityLevel = 'loose' (see NOTES.md, Zoom); this fixture leaves the default strict, so it stays a two-<script> fixture.

---
config:
  layout: elk
  flowchart:
    useMaxWidth: false
  elk:
    nodePlacementStrategy: NETWORK_SIMPLEX
---
flowchart TD
  accTitle: Large connections map for the focus topic
  accDescr: A focus topic with two eras of antecedents and descendants grouped into open subgraphs, every node individually clickable, and a legend explaining that a solid line is a technological connection and a dashed line is a conceptual one.
  subgraph legend["Legend"]
    direction LR
    key1((" ")) -->|technological| key2((" "))
    key3((" ")) -.->|conceptual| key4((" "))
  end
  focus[Focus Topic]
  subgraph pre2010["Antecedents, pre-2010"]
    a1[Antecedent A]
    a2[Antecedent B]
    a3[Antecedent C]
    a4[Antecedent D]
    a5[Antecedent E]
    a6[Antecedent F]
  end
  subgraph post2010["Descendants, post-2010"]
    d1[Descendant W]
    d2[Descendant X]
    d3[Descendant Y]
    d4[Descendant Z]
  end
  a1 --> focus
  a2 --> focus
  a3 --> focus
  a4 -.-> focus
  a5 -.-> focus
  a6 --> focus
  focus --> d1
  focus --> d2
  focus -.-> d3
  focus --> d4
